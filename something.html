Who Are You Sending Flowers To?
Your Romantic Partner
For your partner, romantic and meaningful blooms are always a beautiful choice. Classic roses are timeless, while their favourite flowers or a bouquet in their favourite colour can make the gesture feel even more personal.
Best for: Anniversaries · Valentine's Day · Birthdays · Just Because
A Colleague or Business Partner
For corporate gifting, opt for sophisticated arrangements with a refined colour palette. Minimal, elegant designs are ideal when you want to express appreciation without feeling too personal.
Best for: Congratulations · Thank You · New Business · Corporate Events
A Friend
Colourful bouquets and playful arrangements work beautifully for friends. Choose something that reflects their personality or simply brings a little happiness to their day.
Best for: Birthdays · Congratulations · Get Well Soon
Your Parents
Soft, elegant arrangements in cheerful or sophisticated colours are a lovely way to show appreciation. Consider flowers that feel warm, graceful, and thoughtful rather than overly romantic.
Best for: Mother's Day · Father's Day · Birthdays · Thank You
Someone Going Through a Difficult Time
Let the flowers speak gently. Choose elegant, understated arrangements in soft or calming tones. The gesture is more important than the size — thoughtful flowers can quietly communicate that you're thinking of them.
Best for: Sympathy · Condolences · Get Well Soon · Thinking of You

Care Instructions
Keep away from heat and direct sunlight
Place your flowers in a cool spot away from windows, heating vents, or appliances. Direct sunlight and heat will cause petals to wilt and dry out faster.
Fill up and change water daily
Refresh the vase water every day to prevent bacterial buildup that blocks stems. Top it up to ensure stems are always submerged at least 5–7 cm deep.
Ensure floral foam is never completely dry
If your arrangement uses floral foam, keep it moist at all times by adding water regularly. Dry foam cannot reabsorb water and will cause stems to dehydrate quickly.
Remove wilted blooms or foliage
Promptly trim away any dead or drooping flowers and yellow leaves using clean scissors. Wilted parts drain energy and nutrients from healthy blooms, shortening the arrangement's life.
Occasionally mist your flower
Lightly spray a fine mist of water on the petals and leaves every 1–2 days. This keeps the blooms hydrated and adds humidity, especially in air-conditioned rooms.
Discard water and wash vase every second day
Every other day, empty the vase completely and clean it with mild soap to remove bacteria and residue. A clean vase keeps the water fresher for longer and extends bloom life.
